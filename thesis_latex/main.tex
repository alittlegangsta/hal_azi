\begin{chineseabstract}
固井水泥环承担层间封隔、套管支撑和井筒屏障维持等作用。窜槽具有沿深度延伸和周向局部化特征，需要结合声波传播响应与超声周向成像进行评价。本文研究数据来源于与美国哈里伯顿公司开展的横向合作项目，其中 XSI 阵列声波测井能够记录多接收器全波列，CAST 超声成像测井能够提供深度--方位声阻抗图；然而，现有数据无法建立两类工具绝对方位零点之间的唯一转换关系，直接采用方位像素到声波通道的逐点监督会引入未知循环偏移。

针对上述问题，本文首先核验 XSI、CAST、Relative Bearing 和 Inclination 的数据结构及坐标物理含义。目标井段总体接近直井，Relative Bearing 存在较多跳变，Inclination 只表示井斜大小，不能作为方位校正角。随后，以 CAST 声阻抗构造一维窜槽占比标签和方位向快速傅里叶变换幅值标签。后者利用离散方位序列循环平移只改变傅里叶相位、不改变幅值的性质，降低对绝对方位零点的依赖，在保留环向结构频率组成的同时舍弃绝对方位和相位信息。XSI 第 3 接收环的 8 通道波形经连续小波变换形成 $150\times400\times8$ 时频张量，并由 EfficientNetV2B0 骨干回归 $70\times30$ 的 CAST 派生结构标签。

在带 5~ft 缓冲区的单井深度区间隔离划分下，主要实验测试 MAE 为 0.079025、RMSE 为 0.277019、$R^2$ 为 0.106634、Pearson 相关系数为 0.351950、Spearman 相关系数为 0.480519，测试样本数为 423。模型的 RMSE 和 $R^2$ 优于零值基线，但 MAE 未优于零值基线；模型与训练集中位数基线的 RMSE 接近。逐系数和分严重度分析表明，低阶傅里叶系数误差较大，较高严重度样本存在低估。一维窜槽占比标签对照实验的测试 $R^2$ 为 $-0.011278$。结果说明，声波时频特征对旋转不变 CAST 派生标签具有有限可学习性，但现有证据不支持绝对方位恢复、跨井泛化或工业应用结论。

\chinesekeyword{声波测井，水泥窜槽，方位失配，连续小波变换，傅里叶幅值标签，深度区间隔离验证}
\end{chineseabstract}

\begin{englishabstract}
The cement sheath provides zonal isolation, casing support, and an important well-integrity barrier. Cement channeling is both depth-continuous and azimuthally localized, motivating the joint analysis of acoustic waveforms and circumferential ultrasonic images. Data for this study were obtained through a collaborative project with Halliburton and comprise XSI array acoustic logging and CAST ultrasonic cement evaluation measurements. XSI records multi-receiver full waveforms, whereas CAST provides depth--azimuth acoustic-impedance images. The available metadata, however, do not define a unique transformation between the absolute azimuth zeros of the two tools. Direct bin-to-channel supervision would therefore contain an unknown cyclic offset.

This thesis first verifies the structures and physical meanings of the XSI, CAST, Relative Bearing, and Inclination data. The target interval is nearly vertical, Relative Bearing contains frequent jumps, and Inclination represents tilt magnitude rather than an azimuth-correction angle. Two CAST-derived targets are then constructed: a one-dimensional channeling-proportion label and a rotation-invariant azimuthal Fourier-magnitude label. The latter uses the fact that a cyclic shift of a discrete azimuthal sequence changes Fourier phase but preserves magnitude. It retains the frequency composition of circumferential structure while discarding phase and absolute azimuth, and therefore cannot recover the absolute channeling direction. Eight XSI channels from receiver ring 03 are transformed into a $150\times400\times8$ continuous-wavelet time-frequency tensor, from which an EfficientNetV2B0 backbone predicts a $70\times30$ CAST-derived structural target.

Under a single-well depth-blocked split with 5-ft buffers, the main experiment obtains a test MAE of 0.079025, RMSE of 0.277019, $R^2$ of 0.106634, Pearson correlation of 0.351950, and Spearman correlation of 0.480519 on 423 samples. The model improves RMSE and $R^2$ over a constant-zero predictor but not MAE, and its RMSE is close to that of a predictor formed from training-set medians. Coefficient-wise and severity-wise analyses show larger errors for low-order Fourier coefficients and underestimation of higher-severity samples. The one-dimensional comparison yields a test $R^2$ of $-0.011278$. These results show limited learnability of the rotation-invariant CAST-derived target, but they do not establish absolute-azimuth recovery, cross-well generalization, or industrial readiness.

\englishkeyword{Array acoustic logging, cement channeling, azimuthal mismatch, continuous wavelet transform, azimuthal Fourier magnitude, single-well depth-blocked split}
\end{englishabstract}
