# Research question
Whether arcuate phased-array receiver stations can estimate the azimuth and angular extent of interface-I channeling.

# Data and sample
Three-dimensional finite-difference simulations and artificial experimental wells.

# Instrument or method
AABT 2.0 uses two monopole transmitters and two eight-element circumferential receiver stations; independent and scanning reception are compared.

# Main findings
Scanning reception narrows the directional response and better resolves the designed channel azimuth in the reported experiments.

# Limitations
The instrument has a known coordinate in controlled wells. This does not establish the unknown XSI--CAST zero-angle conversion.

# Relevance to this thesis
Shows why receiver numbering becomes an absolute azimuth only after a reference direction is known.

# Claims supported
Azimuthal cement evaluation requires both directional sensing and a defined reference.

# Claims not supported
It does not justify assigning CAST azimuth bin zero to XSI side A.
