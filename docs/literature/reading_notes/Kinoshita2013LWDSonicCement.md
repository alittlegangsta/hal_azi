# Research question
Under what casing, cement, and collar conditions LWD sonic arrivals can quantify cement bonding.

# Data and sample
Finite-difference simulations and two field or test-well cases.

# Instrument or method
The casing extensional arrival is compared with collar propagation in the attenuator's operating band.

# Main findings
Quantitative sensitivity depends on casing thickness, cement acoustic impedance, tool response, and calibration.

# Limitations
The reported bond-index limits are conditional on the studied tool and cases.

# Relevance to this thesis
Supports coupled tool, casing, cement, and formation effects in acoustic evaluation.

# Claims supported
A single acoustic amplitude is not an instrument-independent measure of cement quality.

# Claims not supported
It does not establish XSI--CAST azimuth registration or the 2.5 threshold.
