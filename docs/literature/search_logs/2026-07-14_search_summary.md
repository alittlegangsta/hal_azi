# Literature search log (2026-07-14)

## Scope and sources

The search covered T1--T10 in the task specification. Discovery used the read-only paperSearch connectors for Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and arXiv. Google Scholar and Sci-Hub were not used. Zotero was used only to verify existing records and local attachments.

## Query families

T1 covered well integrity, cement sheath integrity, channeling, and zonal isolation. T2 and T3 covered CBL/VDL, array sonic, segmented tools, pulse-echo, impedance, and circumferential imaging. T4 covered relative bearing, tool azimuth, and registration. T5 covered CWT and nonstationary acoustic signals. T6 covered the Fourier shift theorem, magnitude, cyclic translation, and Fourier descriptors. T7 covered learning for logs and cement evaluation. T8 and T9 targeted the original EfficientNetV2 and Grad-CAM papers. T10 covered blocked validation and leakage in correlated samples.

## Screening decisions

Unrelated biomedical cement, generic ultrasonic NDT, figure-only DOI records, marketing pages, and unverified reposts were excluded. Search snippets were never treated as paper content. The final set combines foundational sources and 2016--2025 studies, without using Google Scholar, Sci-Hub, or paywall bypass.
